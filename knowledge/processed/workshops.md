## AI Tools Workshop (recurring format)

This is a free, live, beginner-friendly workshop that runs for about one hour on Google Meet, Zoom, or on campus, repeated as different dated editions.

What it covers:
- Comparing leading AI models
- Prompt engineering
- AI tools for students and working professionals
- Workflow automation
- Live Q&A

## Figma × Cursor AI/UI/UX Workshop (recurring format)

This is a separate workshop focused on design-to-code workflows. It must not be merged with the AI Tools Workshop.

Known facts:
- Duration: 1 hour, live
- Platform: Zoom
- Price: free
- Beginner friendly
- Curriculum: Figma fundamentals, why Figma leads the industry, Cursor, AI plugins, Model Context Protocol (MCP) explained, Figma MCP × Cursor / Figma-to-Cursor integration, AI design interpretation, prompt to prototype, design systems and tokens, design to code, live implementation with Cursor converting designs into React/Next.js applications, and Q&A
