# 60-Day Claude AI Challenge

The 60-Day Claude AI Challenge is a free, 60-day build-in-public program focused on prompt engineering and practical AI workflows.

Key points:
- Launch date on the current site: June 1, 2026
- Participants complete one AI-assisted build every day
- Work is documented publicly on LinkedIn and GitHub
- The outcome is a public build log and a stronger portfolio
- Verified completion includes a certificate with QR-based verification

## Official challenge guidelines

Source: `public/documents/ABTalks-60-Day-Challenge-Guidelines.pdf` (present in this repository and linked from the live site — authoritative for this program).

### GitHub repository setup

- Create ONE dedicated GitHub repository — it is the official record of the 60 days
- Organize it day by day: `Day1/`, `Day2/`, `Day3/`, `Day4/` ... through `Day60/`
- Each day's folder holds that day's prompts, outputs, code, notes, and screenshots
- A clean, well-structured repo makes progress easy to review

### Sharing progress on LinkedIn

Each post should clearly cover:
- What was completed and what was built or explored
- Key learnings, insights, and any real-world applications
- Screenshots or a short demo of the result

Every post must tag all three accounts: `@AnilBajpai`, `@ABTalksOnAI`, and `@Anthropic`, so the team can track participation and amplify the work.

### What counts as a submission

A day is complete only when all three steps are done:
1. Work is pushed to the GitHub repository
2. Progress is shared in a LinkedIn post
3. All required tags are included in that post

Missing any one of the three steps means that day's submission may not count.

### Consistency and streaks

- Progress is tracked on a 60-day streak heatmap
- A participant's current streak is based on making an on-time submission every day
- Submissions made late do not count towards the current streak
- Missing a day does not remove a participant from the challenge — the streak resets to 0, but participation continues; the goal is to keep going
- Consistency is weighed when the team considers: goodies, community recognition, and special opportunities

### Certificates

- A verified Certificate of Completion is issued when a participant completes the final **Day 60** submission AND has completed **at least 50 days** in total throughout the challenge.
- Certificates include a unique ID (`ABT-XX-XXXXX`) and a QR code for public verification.
- Missing a day does not remove a participant from the challenge — the streak resets, but participation continues; the goal is to keep going
- Consistency is weighed when the team considers: certificates, goodies, community recognition, and special opportunities

### What makes a strong participant

Participants who make the strongest impact consistently bring:
- Daily participation — an uninterrupted, visible streak
- A clean repository — clear folders, easy to navigate
- Professional documentation — well-written, high-quality posts
- Thoughtful reflection — real learnings, not just outputs

The goal isn't 60 tasks — it's a public record of real growth.

## Status note

The published launch date (June 1, 2026) is in the past relative to the current project date (2026-08-10). Whether the program is a fixed cohort with a closed enrollment window, or a rolling/self-paced 60-day track measured from each participant's own start, is not stated in the guidelines document itself. Do not assume the challenge is closed to new entrants without a current-site confirmation — treat older references to specific challenge dates as historical, but the challenge's mechanics (repo structure, tagging, submission rules) above are the current official rules regardless of cohort timing.
