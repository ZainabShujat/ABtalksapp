## What it is

ABTalks runs a **real-time voice interview conducted by an AI interviewer**.
You speak your answers out loud; the interviewer asks follow-up questions
based on what you actually said, and afterwards you get a written assessment
report.

It is part of the **31-Day AI Cohort** (`/program`). It is not a standalone
practice tool, and it is not open to the general public.

## Who can use it, and how to access it

- You must be a **signed-in AI Cohort program member**. The interview pages
  live under `/program` and are closed to everyone else.
- There is no public or guest version. If you are not in the cohort, the way
  in is to apply to the AI Cohort — see `ai-cohort.md`.
- Interviews unlock at **milestones**, not on demand. Each one opens only
  once you have reached the cohort day it covers.

## The two milestone interviews

- **Day 15 Checkpoint Interview** — covers cohort days 1–15 (through the LLM
  Core, Prompting & Fine-Tuning module).
- **Day 31 Final Interview** — covers the whole programme including the Day
  31 capstone. This is the exit interview.

Each is a **one-time, roughly 15-minute** voice conversation. It is scored
separately from your leaderboard total.

## How the interview runs

- Questions are generated from the cohort curriculum your milestone covers
  and from the work you did — an interview can only ask about material
  inside its own scope.
- The interviewer asks **follow-up questions** based on your answers rather
  than reading from a fixed list.
- If you are stuck on several questions in a row, the interview concludes
  early rather than grinding on.
- Before the session starts you are shown an in-product notice that it is
  **recorded and transcribed for evaluation**.
- Very short attempts are not scored — an interview that ends almost
  immediately is closed as invalid and **does not consume your attempt**, so
  a dropped connection or a closed tab does not cost you the milestone.

## Can I practise with my resume or a job description?

**No.** The interview that exists today is the AI Cohort milestone
interview, and it is scoped to the cohort curriculum. There is currently no
resume-based or job-description-based interview available to users — a
general challenge interview route existed previously and now redirects to
the program dashboard. Do not tell users they can upload a resume or paste a
job description for an interview; if they ask for that, say it is not
currently offered and point them at team@abtalks.in.

## What you get afterwards — the report

A saved assessment report, which you can reopen later. It includes:

- an **overall score out of 10** and a readiness view
- a **competency breakdown**, each scored out of 10
- **module performance**, marked Strong / Developing / Weak, with modules
  the interview did not reach called out as unassessed
- a **skill assessment** section
- **evidence and deep dive** — every question expandable to show the actual
  exchange and why that answer got the score it got
- **agent insights** — behavioural and structural observations across the
  interview

The report is a record of an assessment that already happened; it is stored
once and not recomputed, so it says the same thing every time you open it.
Your report is yours — it is scoped to you and no other member can open it.

## How it connects to hiring

Finishing the AI Cohort unlocks the final interview, and its summary forms
part of what recruiters see on your ranked profile in the talent portal. See
`hiring-and-recruiters.md` — your personal contact details are still not
exposed without your approval.

## AI processing

Interview audio and transcripts are sent to AI providers to generate
feedback, scores and summaries, as stated in the Privacy Policy. Do not put
passwords, card numbers or unrelated sensitive information into an interview
or any other submission.
