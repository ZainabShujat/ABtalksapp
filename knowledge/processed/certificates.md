# ABTalks Certificates — Eligibility, Claiming, Verification

Covers every ABTalks certificate: who gets one, how to claim it, where to
download it, and how anyone (recruiter, college, employer) can verify it.

## Which programs issue certificates

ABTalks currently issues two kinds of certificate:

- **60-Day Claude AI Challenge** — "Certificate of Completion", subtitle "Claude AI Mastery Track". Self-serve: it is issued automatically once you qualify.
- **ViCoDathon 2026 hackathon** — "ViCoDathon 2026", subtitle "India's AI Vibe Coding Hackathon". Issued by the ABTalks team in a batch after the event.

The 60-Day Coding Challenge (AI / DS / SE tracks) gives **completion
recognition and a public portfolio** rather than an automatically issued
PDF certificate — the core outcome there is visible proof of work
(GitHub + LinkedIn) on your ABTalks profile.

Cohort and workshop certificate types exist in the platform but are not
being issued automatically today. If you attended an AI Cohort or a
workshop and need a certificate, email team@abtalks.in.

## Certificates are always free

ABTalks never charges to issue, unlock, release, or verify a certificate.
Every ABTalks program is free to participate in. If anyone asks you for
money to release a certificate, it is a scam — do not pay, and forward the
message to team@abtalks.in.

## How to claim a certificate (step by step)

1. Sign in to ABTalks at `/login` with the same account you used for the
   challenge or hackathon.
2. Make sure your **full name is filled in on your profile** (`/profile`).
   The certificate prints your ABTalks profile full name exactly as saved.
   If it is blank you will see "Complete your profile name before claiming
   your certificate."
3. Go to **`/achievements`** ("Your Achievements"). You can also reach it
   from `/profile` → "View achievements", or from the mobile sidebar menu.
   `/achievements` requires you to be signed in.
4. Opening that page **is** the claim step. There is no separate "claim"
   button and no application form — the moment you are eligible, opening
   `/achievements` issues the certificate and it appears as a card.
5. On the certificate card you can:
   - **View certificate** — opens the public verification page.
   - **Download PDF** — downloads the certificate as a PDF.
   - **Copy verification link** — copies the public link to share on
     LinkedIn, in a resume, or with a recruiter.

If `/achievements` says "No achievements yet", you are not eligible yet (or,
for the hackathon, the team has not issued that batch yet).

## Eligibility — 60-Day Claude AI Challenge certificate

To be issued a Claude Challenge Certificate of Completion you must:

- Be enrolled in the CLAUDE domain challenge, **and**
- Have a submission for **Day 60** (the final day), **and**
- Have **at least 50 days completed** in total across the 60 days.

Missing a few days does not disqualify you — up to 10 missed days are
tolerated, but the Day 60 submission itself is mandatory. Your certificate
records your days completed and your longest streak, and both are shown on
the public verification page.

## Eligibility — ViCoDathon 2026 hackathon certificate

- **Participation certificate:** every registered participant on a team
  whose submission includes **both a repo URL and a live URL** gets one.
  Solo entries count as a team of one.
- Every member of a qualifying team gets their own certificate — leader and
  members alike.
- It records your team name (or "Solo entry") and the problem brief your
  team built against.
- These are issued in a batch by the ABTalks team after judging, not
  instantly on submission. Once issued, they show up on `/achievements`
  exactly like any other certificate.
- **Award certificates:** separate, extra certificates for **Winner, 2nd
  place, 3rd place and Top 5**, each on its own artwork, issued by the
  ABTalks team to the placing teams. An award certificate is issued **in
  addition to** the participation certificate, so a winner has two entries
  on their achievements page. ViCoDathon award certificates carry a date of
  issue of 14 August 2026.

If your team submitted but you have no certificate after the batch is
issued, the usual cause is a missing repo URL or live URL on the
submission. Email team@abtalks.in.

## Certificate ID format

Every certificate has a unique ID in the form `ABT-XX-XXXXX`:

- `CC` — 60-Day Claude Challenge
- `HK` — ViCoDathon hackathon (participation and award certificates alike)
- `CH` — Cohort
- `WS` — Workshop

The last five characters use an unambiguous alphabet (no 0, O, 1, I or L),
so an ID read off a printed certificate is never ambiguous. IDs are not
case-sensitive when you type them into the verification URL.

## How to verify a certificate (recruiters, colleges, employers)

- Every certificate carries a **QR code** and a **unique certificate ID**.
  Scanning the QR opens the verification page directly.
- Or visit **`https://abtalks.in/verify/<CERTIFICATE-ID>`** — for example
  `https://abtalks.in/verify/ABT-CC-7K3PQ`.
- The verification page is **public — no login or ABTalks account needed**.
- A valid certificate shows: "Verified Certificate", recipient name,
  credential title, issue date, certificate ID, status, plus the track /
  days completed / longest streak (challenge) or team and brief
  (hackathon), and an embedded preview of the certificate itself.
- Anyone on that page can download the certificate PDF at
  `https://abtalks.in/verify/<CERTIFICATE-ID>/download`.
- If the ID does not exist, the page says "Certificate not found" — check
  the ID for typos and try again.
- If a certificate has been withdrawn, the page says "This certificate has
  been revoked" and the PDF download is disabled. Revoked certificates
  still appear on the holder's achievements page, marked "Revoked".

## Sharing your certificate

Use the **Copy verification link** button on `/achievements` and paste the
`abtalks.in/verify/...` link into your LinkedIn post, LinkedIn "Licenses &
certifications" credential URL, resume, or portfolio. Because the link is
public and shows the recipient name and issue date, anyone can confirm it
without asking you for the PDF.

## Certificate troubleshooting

**Q: I finished the challenge but `/achievements` shows nothing.**
A: Check that you actually have a Day 60 submission and at least 50 days
completed — both are required. If both are true, email team@abtalks.in with
the email address on your ABTalks account.

**Q: The name on my certificate is wrong / missing.**
A: The certificate uses the full name on your ABTalks profile. Update it at
`/profile`. If a certificate was already issued with the wrong name, email
team@abtalks.in to have it re-issued.

**Q: The download fails with a message about my name.**
A: Certificate PDFs can only print Latin characters. If your profile name
is written entirely in a non-Latin script, the PDF cannot be generated —
change your profile name to its Latin spelling and contact
team@abtalks.in so the certificate can be re-issued.

**Q: Do I need to pay or apply to get my certificate?**
A: No. There is no fee and no application. Certificates are issued
automatically to eligible participants (challenge) or in a batch by the
team (hackathon), and appear on `/achievements`.

**Q: Can I get a certificate for the 60-Day Coding Challenge (AI/DS/SE)?**
A: That track gives completion recognition and a public portfolio of proof
of work rather than an auto-issued PDF certificate. The Claude Challenge is
the track with the automatic verified Certificate of Completion.

**Q: Where do I ask about anything else certificate-related?**
A: team@abtalks.in.
