## How it works (4 steps)

1. **Register** — sign up solo or create a team of up to 3. Under two
   minutes, free.
2. **Join the WhatsApp group** — after registering. Kickoff updates and the
   problem statement land there first.
3. **Build for 48 hours** — Friday kickoff to Sunday deadline. Describe what
   you want, let AI write the code, ship something real.
4. **Submit before the deadline** — late submissions don't count.

## The three required deliverables

- **Public GitHub repo** — full project source, public and cloneable.
  Private repos will not be judged.
- **Live deployed URL** — something the judges can open (Vercel, Netlify, any
  reachable host). A README-only demo does not count.
- **AI-usage log** — a `PROMPTS.md` in the repo, or exported chat
  transcripts. This is how the team verifies the build was genuinely
  vibe-coded.

## Rules

- **Solo or teams of up to 3.** One shareable 6-character team code joins
  teammates; maximum three people total. Duplicate team names are blocked.
- **Open to Indian college students**, 1st year through recent grads. One
  entry per person, enforced by email address.
- **Build starts at kickoff.** No head starts. Anything pre-built must be
  disclosed in your submission notes.
- **Fair play.** Use any AI coding tool. Don't submit someone else's work as
  your own. Be kind in the community chat.

## Judging criteria

- **Product judgment** — did you pick a sharp problem and ship a coherent answer?
- **Prompting skill** — does the AI-usage log show deliberate steering rather
  than blind copy-paste?
- **Polish & deploy** — does it run live, look intentional, and explain itself?

Summarised on the results stage as: originality, polish, and how well you
steered the AI.

## Hackathon FAQ (verbatim from the live page)

**Q: Do I need a team?**
A: No. Solo entries are welcome. If you want teammates, create a team and
share the 6-character code, up to 3 people total.

**Q: What if I can't code?**
A: That's the point of vibe coding. You describe the product; tools like
Cursor or Claude Code write the implementation. Judgment and prompting
matter more than typing speed.

**Q: Is it free?**
A: Yes. Registration and entry are completely free.

**Q: What counts as vibe coding?**
A: You steer with natural language and AI writes most of the code.
Hand-typing every line defeats the theme — the AI-usage log is checked.

**Q: Can I use a template?**
A: Starter templates and boilerplates are fine if you disclose them. The
bulk of the product should be built during the 48 hours.

**Q: How are winners picked?**
A: Judges weigh originality, how well you steered the AI (via your prompt
log), and whether the live deploy actually works.
