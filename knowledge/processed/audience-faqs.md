## Eligibility — who can join ABTalks programs

ABTalks programs are open to college students at any year of study — first
year through final year — across any branch or degree (BTech, BE, or
otherwise), as well as recent graduates and working professionals. There is
no minimum year-of-study requirement for the 60-Day Coding Challenge, the
60-Day Claude AI Challenge, or the AI Cohort. A first-year, second-year,
third-year, or final-year BTech student can all participate; so can someone
who has already graduated or is currently employed.

Program-specific eligibility notes:
- 60-Day Coding Challenge, 60-Day Claude AI Challenge: open to any year of
  study, any branch, no prerequisites required to start.
- AI Cohort: open to students and recent grads who meet the stated
  prerequisites (Python fundamentals, SQL, Git/GitHub, REST APIs, JavaScript
  & React basics) — see `ai-cohort.md`. Year of study isn't itself a
  restriction; the prerequisites are what determine readiness.
- Vibe Code Hackathon (ViCodathon): eligibility is Indian college students;
  see `vicodathon.md` for team-size and submission requirements.

Source: `abtalks.md` ("Audience"), `claude-challenge.md`, and
`testimonials.md` (the Claude Challenge testimonial roster includes a 20+
year IT leader, confirming the program isn't restricted by career stage
either).

## For students

**Q: What's the difference between the 60-Day Coding Challenge and the
60-Day Claude AI Challenge?**
A: The 60-Day Coding Challenge lets you pick a broad domain (Software
Engineering / Data Science / AI) and build daily projects in it. The 60-Day
Claude AI Challenge is specifically about mastering Claude and prompt
engineering, with a 10-day capstone to ship a deployed product. Both are
free.

**Q: Which program should I pick if I want a job?**
A: If you already meet the prerequisites (Python, SQL, Git, React basics),
the 31-Day AI Cohort is the most jobs-oriented — it ends in a real-time AI
voice interview and recruiter visibility. If you're earlier in your journey,
do the 60-Day Coding Challenge in your target track to build a portfolio
first.

**Q: Do I need a laptop with a GPU?**
A: No. The AI Cohort explicitly asks for ≥8 GB RAM only, and uses
Ollama / Groq / Chroma — all free-tier / local. No paid API keys required.

**Q: Do I need to know how to code?**
A: For the 60-Day Coding Challenge and AI Cohort, yes — pick the track that
matches your current level. For the 60-Day Claude AI Challenge, you can
start as a beginner; the daily tasks are prompts you run, not code you write
from scratch (though the outputs are apps you'll deploy).

**Q: What if I miss a day?**
A: You can keep going, but the missed day breaks your streak. Submissions
follow IST day boundaries.

**Q: How much time per day does it take?**
A: 31-Day AI Cohort: roughly 2–4 hours/day. 60-Day Claude Challenge: varies
by task, generally 30–90 minutes. 60-Day Coding Challenge: designed as "one
task a day."

**Q: What are the community rules?**
A: Foul language, harassment, or harmful messaging results in a permanent
ban. Plagiarism or platform misuse results in a 60-day challenge ban. See
`community.md`.

**Q: How do I join the community?**
A: Use the "Join now" link on the official website for the WhatsApp
community (invite links rotate, so there's no fixed URL to give out here).
Also Discord, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, X — all under `abtalksonai`. See
`socials-and-contact.md`.

**Q: How do I apply for placements or internships through ABTalks?**
A: Email your resume, cover letter, and anything else relevant (portfolio
link, GitHub, LinkedIn, project links) to team@abtalks.in. The team reviews
applications and routes strong profiles to the hiring partner network.
Openings themselves are also shared inside the ABTalks WhatsApp community,
so it's worth joining if you haven't already.

**Q: Do I need to have finished a program before applying?**
A: No — you can email team@abtalks.in anytime. But a completed program gives
you public artifacts to point to, which is exactly the signal recruiters in
the network look for.

**Q: What should go in the application email?**
A: Resume, cover letter, and links that prove work — GitHub, deployed
projects, LinkedIn build log, ABTalks profile, portfolio site — plus
anything else material to the role you're targeting.

## For recruiters / hiring partners

**Q: What talent does ABTalks produce?**
A: Indian college students and early-career professionals who ship public
artifacts through the programs described in `programs.md` — daily builds,
deployed projects, and (for the Claude Challenge capstone) a full versioned
v1.0.0 release with documentation. See
`claude-challenge-curriculum.md` for the graduate skills matrix.

**Q: How do I access candidates?**
A: ABTalks maintains a hiring partner network (100+ hiring partners, per
`community.md`). Contact team@abtalks.in to be onboarded. Candidates apply
by emailing their resume, cover letter, and work links to the same address,
so profiles arrive pre-curated.

**Q: How do openings get to candidates?**
A: Openings shared by partners are announced inside the ABTalks WhatsApp
community. team@abtalks.in handles the intake side.

**Q: What signal does an ABTalks profile give?**
A: Consistency (a visible streak across the program's duration), shipping
ability (deployed URLs, not just repos), and — for Claude Challenge
graduates — SDLC discipline (versioned releases, docs, retrospectives).

**Q: Are candidates screened?**
A: Each program has some form of verification — GitHub artifact checks and
a real-time AI voice interview for the AI Cohort, and community rules that
permanently ban plagiarism across all programs.

## For working professionals

**Q: Is ABTalks only for students?**
A: The 60-Day Claude AI Challenge is open to anyone. The 60-Day Coding
Challenge is generally described as for college students, but the Claude
Challenge testimonial roster (`testimonials.md`) includes a 20+ year IT
leader, so it isn't a hard restriction in practice for that program.

**Q: I already have a job. What do I get out of this?**
A: Structured daily practice with the modern AI stack, plus shipped
artifacts and (for the Claude Challenge) a deployed capstone product you can
point to. Several testimonials come from mid-career professionals
repositioning into AI/GenAI work.

**Q: Can I do this alongside a full-time job?**
A: Yes. The 60-Day Claude Challenge tasks are typically 30–90 minutes; the
AI Cohort assumes roughly 2–4 hours/day for 31 days.
