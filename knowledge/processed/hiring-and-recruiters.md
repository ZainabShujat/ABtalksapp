# Hiring on ABTalks — Scout, the Recruiter Network & Candidate Consent

This covers the employer/recruiter side of ABTalks and what candidates need
to know about how their profile reaches companies. Source of truth: the live
public pages `/` (homepage), `/hire` and the talent portal.

## The pitch

ABTalks does evidence-based hiring rather than resume- and interview-only
hiring. Companies see what a candidate actually built and shipped on the
platform — dated commits, deployed URLs, completed missions, streaks,
interview scores — instead of a self-written resume.

Homepage headline numbers: 10k+ people on the platform, 100+ companies in
the recruiter network, 15+ profiles shared with consent.

## Scout (`/hire`)

`/hire` is the recruiter entry point, a chat-style tool called **Scout**.
You describe the role in plain language — stack, seniority, salary band
(e.g. ₹5–10 LPA, ₹10–20 LPA, ₹20–35 LPA) — and Scout matches candidates
against verified platform evidence rather than resumes. You can start
exploring on `/hire` without an ABTalks account; saving requests and
shortlists requires an approved recruiter account.

Each match can be opened as an **evidence resume** (`/hire/evidence`) —
platform-verified evidence for that match, not a self-written CV.

## Candidate consent — what companies see and when

This is the rule candidates ask about most:

- Before you approve a share, a company sees only an **anonymised
  performance summary**: score band, stack and cohort.
- Your **name, contact details and repositories stay hidden until you
  approve the share**.
- Participant phone numbers and entry details are never exposed to
  recruiters.
- Nobody placed through the recruiter network ever pays a placement fee.

## The talent portal (`/talent`)

Recruiters who complete the AI Cohort talent flow use the talent portal to
browse ranked participant profiles — mission portfolios plus AI interview
summaries. Recruiter accounts register at `/talent/register` and are
reviewed before approval (`/talent/pending` while waiting); approved
recruiters get a shortlist view.

## For candidates: how to become discoverable

1. Finish a program (60-Day Coding Challenge, Claude Challenge, or the
   31-Day AI Cohort) so there is dated public proof of work on your profile.
2. Keep the proof clean — public GitHub repos, live deploys, LinkedIn posts.
3. AI Cohort finishers additionally unlock a 15-minute real-time AI voice
   interview whose summary recruiters can see.
4. Approve a share when a company requests your full profile.

## Jobs board

`/jobs` is the ABTalks jobs board, visible once you are signed in.

## Custom cohorts for companies

Companies with a niche requirement can have a cohort built around it: send
the role, stack and timeline, and ABTalks designs a challenge for it —
usually running within three to four weeks. Contact team@abtalks.in.
