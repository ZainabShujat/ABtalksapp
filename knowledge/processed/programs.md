# Programs

ABTalks programs are built around shipping real work in public: daily or timed builds, documented via verified proof-of-work trails (see `abtalks.md`). Rules for submission, GitHub, or LinkedIn vary by specific program.

## Program index

- 60-Day Coding Challenge: 60 days, daily task, self-paced, tracked
- 60-Day Claude AI Challenge: 60 days, daily prompt-engineering tasks
- AI Cohort: 31 days, 8 phases, application-gated, 2–4 hrs/day
- Vibe Code Hackathon: 48 hours, solo or teams of 1–3
- Free AI Bootcamp: 1 hour, live on Google Meet, recurring — relationship to the AI Tools Workshop is **unresolved**; see `workshops.md` and `knowledge/metadata/facts.json` (`free-ai-bootcamp-vs-ai-tools-workshop`). Do not present this as a confirmed, independent program distinct from the AI Tools Workshop.

## 60-Day Coding Challenge

- Duration: 60 days, one task per day
- Tracks: Software Engineering, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence
- Format: self-paced, tracked via streaks/heatmap
- Proof of work: GitHub push + LinkedIn post per day
- Cost: free
- Full detail: `coding-challenge.md`

## 60-Day Claude AI Challenge

- Duration: 60 days, one AI-assisted build per day
- Focus: prompt engineering and practical AI workflows
- Proof of work: one dedicated GitHub repo (`Day1/` … `Day60/` folders) plus a LinkedIn post per day tagging `@AnilBajpai`, `@ABTalksOnAI`, and `@Anthropic`
- A day only counts when all three are done: GitHub push, LinkedIn post, required tags
- Cost: free; verified completion includes a QR-verified certificate
- Full detail: `claude-challenge.md`

## AI Cohort

- Duration: 31 days end-to-end, 8 gated phases, roughly 2–4 hours/day
- Focus: building and deploying a production-grade enterprise AI chatbot (RAG, embeddings, agents, MCP, guardrails, Docker, Kubernetes, evaluation, deployment)
- Entry: application-gated
- Cost: free; uses free tools (Ollama, Groq, Chroma)
- Full detail: `ai-cohort.md`

## Vibe Code Hackathon (ViCodathon)

- Duration: 48 hours
- Team size: solo or teams of 1–3
- Submission requirements: public GitHub repo, live deployed URL, AI-usage log
- Cost: free to enter
- Status: the most recent known editions (ViCodathon 2026, and the Aug 7, 2026 "48-Hour AI Hackathon") are both historical/completed relative to the current project date
- Full detail: `vicodathon.md` and `events.md`

## Free AI Bootcamp / AI Tools Workshop

- Format: free, live, ~1 hour, beginner-friendly, on Google Meet or Zoom, recurring
- Content: comparing leading AI models, prompt engineering, AI tools, workflow automation, live Q&A
- Relationship status: **UNRESOLVED** — whether "Free AI Bootcamp" and "AI Tools Workshop" are the same offering under two names, or genuinely separate, is not confirmed. Do not assert either way.
- Full detail: `workshops.md`
