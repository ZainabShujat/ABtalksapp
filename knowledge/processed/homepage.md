## Cost — the precise current wording

The homepage FAQ says: **most challenges are free to join. Paid cohorts are
always priced up front**, and anyone placed through the recruiter network
**never pays a placement fee**.

So the accurate answer to "is ABTalks free?" is: the community and the
flagship challenges — the 60-Day Coding Challenge, the 60-Day Claude AI
Challenge, the 31-Day AI Cohort, the hackathon and the public workshops —
are free to join, and there is never a placement fee. Not every future
cohort is guaranteed free; any paid cohort states its price up front before
you sign up. Do not claim that absolutely everything on ABTalks is free
forever.

## Who it is for

Being a student or a developer is **not** required. Designers, data people
and career switchers run the same cohorts. The only requirement is that you
ship something that can be evaluated.

## What companies see before a candidate consents

An anonymised performance summary — score band, stack and cohort. Name,
contact details and repositories stay hidden until the candidate approves
the share. See `hiring-and-recruiters.md`.

## Custom cohorts

Companies with a niche requirement can ask for a cohort built around their
role, stack and timeline — usually running within three to four weeks.
