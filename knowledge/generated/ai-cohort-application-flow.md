# AI Cohort Application Flow — Live Content ("/ai-cohort-register/apply", "/ai-cohort-india/apply")

The 5-step application form applicants fill out after the intro flow on
/ai-cohort-register or /ai-cohort-india. Same step structure for both the
US and India editions.

## Steps
1. **Personal Information** — Tell us about yourself. This helps us personalise your cohort experience and communicate program updates.
2. **Professional Background** — Help us understand your experience level. Instructors use this to calibrate examples and mentorship to your context.
3. **Your Story** — This is the most important section. We want to understand your motivation, your goals, and what you plan to do with what you learn.
4. **Commitment & Readiness** — This program demands real effort. We ask every applicant to confirm these commitments before we review their application.
5. **Review & Submit** — Review your answers carefully before submitting your application.

## Commitments an applicant must confirm (US edition)
- I can commit at least 2 hours per day for the full 30-day program.
- I will attend all live sessions and complete the assigned live projects.
- I understand a 30-minute pre-cohort call is required before acceptance.
- I am currently based in the United States.
- I am ready for an intensive, hands-on learning challenge with real accountability.

## Commitments an applicant must confirm (India edition)
- I can commit at least 2 hours per day for the full 30-day program.
- I will attend all live sessions and complete the assigned live projects.
- I understand a 30-minute pre-cohort call is required before acceptance.
- I am ready for an intensive, hands-on learning challenge with real accountability.

Note: the India edition has no "based in the United States" commitment —
that is the only difference between the two editions' commitment lists.
